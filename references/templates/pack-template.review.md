# Review Guidance: __PACK_NAME__

Guidance for the agent that annotates items the rules did not flag.

## What To Look For

- Urgent situations described in plain words instead of the rule phrases.
- Urgent facts buried in a long description, a log or a pasted message.
- Typing errors in the key words.

## Rules For Annotations

1. Quote exact words, character for character; 1 to 4 short quotes of at least 3 characters.
2. Only raise; never lower. Use a pack rule id with its own tier, or `"LLM"`.
3. Negated and historical mentions are not urgent.
4. Leave out items you would not raise. Never write a list of items that can wait.
